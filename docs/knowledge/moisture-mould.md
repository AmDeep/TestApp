# Moisture, dampness and mould

Moisture control begins by finding and correcting the water source. Common pathways include roof or plumbing leaks, condensation on cold surfaces, foundation seepage, poor drainage and inadequate exhaust. Document location, extent, timing and recurrence. Do not disturb suspect hazardous materials or undertake large remediation without qualified advice. Drying and cleaning methods depend on material, area and contamination.
